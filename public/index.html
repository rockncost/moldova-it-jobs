How are jobs selected?

Local rules read Romanian, Russian and English vacancy text. Role titles and actual duties establish categories. Experience requirements, optional qualifications, student alternatives and work conditions are assessed separately.

Beginner matches have an explicit junior, internship, student or no-experience signal and verified vacancy text. Stretch roles state an experience minimum below two years. Needs checking contains beginner adverts with specific questions about experience, degrees, internship pay or missing details. Other roles contains adverts without explicit beginner signals. Required experience of two years or more, senior roles, unrelated work, unpaid programmes, mandatory relocation abroad, courses and advertised training fees are excluded.

The student welcome badge is omitted when prior work experience is mentioned, including preferred experience. Optional experience remains optional when deciding beginner eligibility. Salary is shown when an amount is stated; other adverts show “Salary amount not stated”. Duplicate adverts share one card, chosen by verified details, the latest detail check and completeness, with every source linked.

Underlined tags and role labels show the original supporting sentence or source field. Evidence keeps the advert’s language and wording. A skill tag means the skill is mentioned; read the evidence to see whether it is required or preferred.

These labels describe the advert; they are not a guarantee of suitability, payment or hiring. Confirm pay and training conditions with the employer. A flexible schedule or part-time work is tagged only when stated.
