How are jobs selected?

Local rules read Romanian, Russian and English vacancy text. Role titles and actual duties establish categories. Experience requirements, optional qualifications, student alternatives and work conditions are assessed separately.

Beginner matches have an explicit junior, internship, student or no-experience signal and verified vacancy text. Stretch roles ask for up to one year of experience. Unclear requirements, completed degrees and mixed seniority go to Needs checking. Required experience of two years or more, senior roles, unrelated work, unpaid programmes, mandatory relocation abroad, courses and advertised training fees are excluded.

These labels describe the advert; they are not a guarantee of suitability, payment or hiring. “Employer training” does not guarantee free training. No stated fee means no fee was found in the text; confirm pay and any training conditions with the employer. A flexible schedule or part-time work is tagged only when stated.
